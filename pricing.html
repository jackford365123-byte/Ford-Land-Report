Questions

Good to know before you buy

How do I get back into a report I already paid for?

After payment, you're unlocked automatically on the device you paid from. To access it from a different device, use the access code option on the report page — reach out to support@fordlandreport.com with your payment confirmation and we'll get you one.

Can I cancel my Unlimited Monthly subscription?

Yes, anytime, directly through Stripe's billing portal link in your payment confirmation email — no need to contact us, though we're glad to help if something doesn't work as expected.

What's the difference between the free preview and a paid report?

The free preview confirms your address and parcel boundary, plus real public data like flood zone, climate, and hazard information. A paid report unlocks the full interactive map, every computed terrain layer, soil and agriculture guidance, and the composite scoring — the analysis that's actually proprietary to this tool.

Do I need to create an account?

No separate login or password — payment itself unlocks the report on your device. If you need help accessing a report you've paid for, email support@fordlandreport.com directly.

Is this a refund or trial?

Single reports are a one-time purchase; Unlimited Monthly can be canceled anytime with no further charges. For a specific refund request, email support@fordlandreport.com and we'll sort it out directly.
