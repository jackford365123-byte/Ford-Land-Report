What It Does

The kind of thing that's cheap to check now and expensive to discover later.

An insurance bill you didn't budget for. A well that costs three times what you expected. A "flat, easy" building site that's actually in the flood zone. This is what finding out early looks like.

01

Live Satellite Map & Parcel Confirmation

See the exact property boundary on satellite imagery before you make any decision based on it — not a neighboring lot, not a guess.

02

Flood Risk, Whole-Parcel

A flood zone discovered after closing means surprise insurance costs, or a build site that was never viable. Know the real risk before you buy, not after.

03

Best Building Site — and Where Not To

The flattest, easiest-looking spot on a property is sometimes inside the flood zone. Know exactly where to build, and where not to, before you commit to a location.

04

Drainage, Near and Far

Trace exactly where seasonal flooding is coming from — even from well beyond the property line — before it becomes a dispute or a repair bill.

05

Well Depth From Real Nearby Wells

A well that needs to go 300 feet instead of 80 is a five-figure difference. Know the real range before you budget for one.

06

Wildfire Risk & Fire History

Insurers increasingly price wildfire risk in directly. Know your real exposure, and this land's actual fire history, before it's a surprise at underwriting.

07

Estimated Carrying Capacity

Buying land expecting to run 30 head when it only supports 12 is an expensive assumption. Know the real number this property can actually support.

08

Income Potential — Hay, Livestock, Crops & Leases

Real dollar ranges computed from this property's actual acreage and rainfall — hay production, livestock, cash crops, hunting and solar leases, even CRP conservation payments. Know what this land could realistically earn before you buy it, not after.

09

Solar Potential, Mapped Point by Point

Know exactly where panels, a garden, or an orchard will actually perform best on this property — not a guess based on which way the road happens to face.

10

Climate, Frost & Freeze Risk

The difference between a crop or livestock choice that thrives and one that's a costly mistake to learn about after you've already planted or bought.

11

Livestock, Forage & Native Species

Specific breed recommendations across ten species, matched to what this exact property can actually support — not generic advice.

12

Oil & Gas Wells, Invasive Species & Emergency Services

The kind of nearby infrastructure and environmental detail most listing sheets skip entirely — easy to miss, expensive to discover after you've already committed.

13

Composite Scores & Side-by-Side Comparison

Homestead Fit, Flood Resilience, Workability, and Privacy at a glance, plus the ability to compare two properties side by side before deciding between them.
